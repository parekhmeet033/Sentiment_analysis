ReviewLens

A Logistic Regression model that reads a movie review and tells you, in plain terms, whether the writer liked the film or not.

Trained on 50,000 IMDB reviews using TF-IDF text features, ReviewLens turns raw opinions into a clear Positive or Negative call — instantly.
